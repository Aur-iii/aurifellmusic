Written with experienced vocal ensembles in mind, this arrangement leans into colourful close harmony, unusual voice movement, and some fairly adventurous leaps, drawing inspiration from the sound of Gene Puerling and The Hi-Lo’s. 
It definitely isn’t a beginner arrangement, but every part was written with singability in mind. A challenging but rewarding choice for jazz choirs and vocal groups looking for a rich, sophisticated setting of this beautiful standard.
